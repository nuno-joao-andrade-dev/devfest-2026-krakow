# Wawel Royal Castle & Cathedral Official Admission & Visitor Information

## Current Admission Prices (2026 Season)

All exhibitions at Wawel Hill operate on individual ticket quotas with timed entry. Advance reservation is strongly recommended.

### Cathedral & Royal Crypts

*   **Cathedral Royal Crypts & Sigismund Bell (Dzwon Zygmunta):**
    *   Regular: 25 PLN
    *   Reduced (students/seniors): 18 PLN
    *   Includes access to Royal Crypts, the Sigismund Bell Tower, and the Cathedral Museum.
*   **Main Nave of Cathedral:** Free entry for prayer and quiet contemplation (restricted during holy mass).

### Free Admission Policy

*   **Free Admission Days:** Mondays (November – March: 9:30 AM – 1:00 PM; April – October: 9:30 AM – 1:00 PM for selected exhibitions). Free tickets must be collected in person at the ticket office on the morning of the visit. Quotas are strictly limited.
*   **Children under 7 years old:** Free admission (complimentary pass required at ticket counter).

## Opening Hours & Seasonal Schedule

### High Season (April 1 – October 31)
*   **Grounds & Courtyard:** 06:00 AM – 8:00 PM daily.
*   **Exhibition Halls:**
    *   Tuesday – Sunday: 09:30 AM – 6:00 PM (Last admission at 5:00 PM).
    *   Monday: 09:30 AM – 1:00 PM (Limited exhibitions only).
*   **Dragon's Den:** 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM daily.

### Low Season (November 1 – March 31)
*   **Grounds & Courtyard:** 06:30 AM – 5:00 PM daily.
*   **Exhibition Halls:**
    *   Tuesday – Sunday: 09:00 AM – 5:00 PM (Last admission at 4:00 PM).
    *   Monday: 09:30 AM – 1:00 PM (Free admission quota).
*   **Dragon's Den:** Closed for winter hibernation of safety reasons.
