# Kraków Tourist Services, Emergency Contacts & Visitor Facilities

## Emergency & Urgent Assistance Lines

In Poland, standard European emergency numbers and dedicated municipal services are operational 24/7 with English and Polish speaking operators.

*   **Integrated European Emergency Number:** `112` (Directs to dispatch for police, medical, and fire rescue)
*   **Police (Policja):** `997`
*   **Medical Emergency & Ambulance (Pogotowie Ratunkowe):** `999`
*   **Fire Service (Straż Pożarna):** `998`
*   **Municipal City Guard (Straż Miejska):** `986` (Handles public order, vehicle towing, lost and found, and pedestrian safety)
*   **Tourist Police Helpline (English):** `+48 12 615 72 29`
*   **24/7 Tourist Medical Help Center (Szpital Uniwersytecki emergency clinic):** `+48 12 400 10 00` (ul. Jakubowskiego 2, Kraków)

## Official InfoKraków Visitor Centers

Kraków operates an official municipal network of visitor information centers (InfoKraków) offering free maps, event tickets, verified guide bookings, and city passes.

1.  **InfoKraków Sukiennice (Main Market Square):**
    *   Location: Rynek Główny 1/3 (Cloth Hall ground floor arcade)
    *   Phone: `+48 12 354 27 16`
    *   Email: `sukiennice@infokrakow.pl`
    *   Hours: Daily 09:00 AM – 7:00 PM
2.  **InfoKraków Wyspiański Pavilion:**
    *   Location: Plac Wszystkich Świętych 2 (Near the City Hall)
    *   Phone: `+48 12 354 27 23`
    *   Email: `wyspianski@infokrakow.pl`
    *   Hours: Daily 09:00 AM – 5:00 PM
3.  **InfoKraków Kazimierz (Jewish Quarter):**
    *   Location: ul. Józefa 7, 31-056 Kraków
    *   Phone: `+48 12 354 27 28`
    *   Email: `jozefa@infokrakow.pl`
    *   Hours: Daily 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM
4.  **InfoKraków Main Railway Station (Kraków Główny):**
    *   Location: ul. Pawia 5a (Main station underground concourse)
    *   Phone: `+48 12 354 27 10`
    *   Hours: Daily 08:00 AM – 7:00 PM
5.  **InfoKraków John Paul II Airport Balice:**
    *   Location: International Arrivals Hall, level 0
    *   Phone: `+48 12 354 27 15`
    *   Hours: Daily 08:00 AM – 10:00 PM

## Certified Guide Services & Excursions

*   **Federation of Polish Guides Association (Koło Przewodników Miejskich w Krakowie):**
    *   Phone: `+48 12 422 69 49`
    *   Website: `https://przewodnicy.krakow.pl`
    *   Certified multilingual guided walks: Royal Route, Wawel Hill, Kazimierz, and Nowa Huta.
*   **Electric Golf Cart Eco-Tours (Stare Miasto & Kazimierz):**
    *   Official stand: Plac Św. Ducha & ul. Sienna
    *   Authorized operator line: `+48 501 123 456`
    *   Pre-recorded audioguides available in 26 languages.
*   **Vistula River Cruise Gondolas & Ships (Wawel Footbridge Pier):**
    *   Pier location: Bulwar Czerwieński (directly beneath Wawel Castle hill)
    *   Reservation: `+48 12 422 08 55`
    *   Operates April through October every 30 minutes between 10:00 AM and 8:00 PM.

    *   Kraków Główny Railway Station (Pawia 5a, Level -1).
